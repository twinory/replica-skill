What to try

  • Tap Video on the home screen. The page loads with the ad slot already gone, because the request was blocked before the page was drawn.
  • Tap App view at the bottom right. The browser chrome slides away, the app banner disappears, and a bottom tab bar appears. Tap the small bar at the very top edge to come back.
  • Tap the shield in the address bar to turn blocking off for that site, then reload. The ad slots come back. That is the honest test.
  • Tap Music to see the DRM case. On iPhone the web player cannot decrypt it, so Husk offers the real app instead of a black screen.
  • Switch between iPhone and Android. Same app, same lists, each using the engine that ships with the phone.

What this demo is

Static HTML that behaves like the Flutter app in browser/lib. It is here so you can feel the two transitions without installing anything. The sites inside it are invented stand-ins, not the real services.
